How to repair a garage door hit by a car?

By Goat Garage Door Service Editorial Team  ·  5 min read

TL;DR

After a car hits a garage door in Fishers, first check for safety hazards, then assess panel, track, spring, cable and opener damage. Small panel dents and misaligned tracks are often repairable; full panel replacement, spring or off track repairs usually need a professional.

Start by making safety your first action: in Fishers, disconnect the garage door opener, keep people and pets away, and do not try to operate a door that is jammed, crooked or sagging. Next, look for the five common problem areas: panels, tracks, rollers, springs and cables, and the opener itself. An accurate assessment separates quick cosmetic fixes from repairs that carry risk and need a technician. Photograph every angle of the damage for insurance and repair records. If the door is still connected to the opener and moves unevenly, that indicates a balance or spring issue. If the door will not move and the track is visibly bent, the door may be off track and require special tools to reset. How do I assess damage after a car hits my garage door?

A structured quick inspection tells you what to do next. First, look for obvious risks: broken glass, hanging hardware, oil or spring fragments. Then check these parts: - Panels: note dents, cracks, or separation along seams. - Tracks: check for visible bends or kinks and whether rollers sit in the track. - Springs: look for a gap in torsion springs or a visibly broken extension spring. - Cables: inspect for fraying or detachment from drums. - Opener: does the motor run without moving the door, or does the opener stop under load. For each item mark it as safe, needs temporary stabilization, or unsafe. Typical outcomes: - Cosmetic dent in a single panel, safe short term. - Bent track or off-track door, unsafe to operate. - Broken spring or cable, immediate professional repair required. Use photos and a short written note to record your findings for insurance and to share with technicians.

Factors that change the final price include the severity of structural damage, whether parts must be ordered, panel material and color matching, and whether the work is performed as emergency service. Can I repair a dented garage door panel myself?

Small cosmetic dents in lightweight panels are sometimes repairable by a confident DIYer, but the decision depends on panel material and your tools. Stamped steel panels can be pulled with suction tools or panel beaters, then sanded and repainted. Aluminum panels are softer and may crease, making a replacement often simpler and visually better. Wood panels require careful matching and finishing, which can be time consuming. If matching finish matters or the dent interferes with seals or door tracking, replacement is more practical. DIY risks include creating a seal problem that lets weather and pests in, or inadvertently misaligning the panel so rollers bind. If you attempt a DIY fix: - Disconnect power to the opener. - Support the door in the raised position or secure it with clamps before working. - Repair only one panel at a time and recheck balance after reinstallation. Stop and call a technician if the door feels heavy, binds, or the springs show issues.

When should I call a pro in Fishers?

Call a professional any time the damage affects door balance, springs, tracks, cables or opener function. Specific signs include a door that hangs crooked, a visible broken torsion spring, detached cables, or a door that moves erratically with the opener. If you have structural concerns where the door frame or header may be compromised, a pro can assess load paths and safety. For Fishers homeowners, consider pro intervention when: - The repair involves springs or high tension parts. - The door is off track or there is frame deformation. - An opener runs but the door does not move, or vice versa. A local professional can also advise whether panel replacement will match existing finish or if a larger section upgrade is more cost effective. If you need immediate safety help, look for a service that offers emergency repairs and gives a clear estimate before starting work.

Warning signs you must not try to repair

If you see any of these, do not operate the door and get professional help.

  • Door hangs crooked or is off track
    Do not try to force or lift; disengage the opener and call a professional
  • A spring is visibly broken
    Do not try to replace or adjust springs yourself due to high stored energy
  • Cables are frayed or detached
    Avoid using the door; broken cables can cause sudden uncontrolled movement
  • Opener runs but door does not move
    Disconnect opener and have a technician inspect drive gears and mounting

Common repairs after a vehicle impact

After a vehicle impact technicians typically perform one or more of these repairs: - Panel replacement or repair when dents or cracks compromise sealing and appearance. - Track straightening or replacement for bent tracks that prevent smooth travel. - Roller replacement if rollers are damaged or seized. - Spring replacement when torsion or extension springs break or lose tension. - Cable replacement when cables are frayed, slipped, or detached. - Opener repair when drive gears, sprockets or mounts are damaged by sudden load. The order of work usually follows safety first, then restore basic function, then finish cosmetic matching. For commercial doors the components are heavier and often require lifts or multiple technicians. Frequently asked questions

How to repair a garage door hit by a car?

Begin with safety: disconnect the opener and keep clear. Assess panels, tracks, springs, cables and the opener, and photograph damage. Minor panel dents can sometimes be repaired; bent tracks, broken springs or detached cables require a trained technician. Obtain a written estimate before work begins.

How much does it cost to fix a garage door dent?

Cost depends on panel material and access. Small dents in lightweight panels are generally low cost, while full panel replacement or color matching raises price. Additional damage to tracks, springs or rollers increases total cost. Ask for a written estimate to compare options.

What is the new trend in garage doors?

Current trends emphasize energy efficient insulated panels, slimline modern windows, and low-maintenance composite materials. Many homeowners also choose updated openers with smart controls for convenience and diagnostic features.

Is it worth repairing a garage door?

Repair is worth it when damage is limited to single panels, rollers or minor track bending and the frame and springs remain sound. If structural components, multiple panels, or safety systems are compromised, replacement may be more cost effective long term. Get a professional assessment to decide.
